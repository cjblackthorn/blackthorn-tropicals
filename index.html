Care library

Grow With Confidence

Tropical plants reward consistency. Start with the current stage, then tune light, water, humidity, and support as the root system expands.

01

Light

Bright indirect light keeps variegation strong without scorching cream sectors.

02

Water

Let the top layer breathe before watering. Roots want oxygen as much as moisture.

03

Roots

Root health leads every sale. Pot up only when roots can hold the next container.

04

Support

A pole or plank encourages larger leaves, stronger petioles, and mature fenestration.
